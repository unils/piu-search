Piu Search is an app search for Android, built for Japanese. It finds installed apps by their Japanese readings, so "のりかえ", "乗り換え" and "norikae" all find the same transit app.

It also finds apps by genre (e.g. "ちず" for map apps) and by developer, and learns from what you open. It is not a home screen replacement; open it from the digital assistant gesture or a Quick Settings tile.

Piu Search has no internet permission and does not collect any data. Everything runs on the device. No Google Play services required.

The app and its description are mainly in Japanese.
